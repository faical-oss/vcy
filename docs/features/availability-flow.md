# Availability flow (hotel and experience detail pages)

Last updated: 2026-11-25

## Purpose
Prices and availability appear only once the visitor has a complete, valid search. Until then the pages show "Select your dates to see prices" and the room types for reference. The same components serve `/hotels/h/<slug>` and `/experiences/e/<slug>`. Mocked: no real endpoint exists yet.

## States
| State | Booking card | Price header | Rooms tab |
|---|---|---|---|
| idle (no complete search) | "Check availability" (opens the date picker, no API call) | "Select your dates to see prices" | reference rooms + notes |
| loading | "Checking availability…" (disabled) | skeleton | skeleton rows |
| ready (at least one room) | "Reserve now" (goes to checkout) | "From $X per night / per person" (cheapest available) | exact prices, sold-out rooms marked |
| none | "Reserve now" (disabled) | "No rooms available" banner | message + Retry |
| error | "Reserve now" (disabled) + Retry | "Prices unavailable" banner | message + Retry |

- **Rooms tab:** experiences show display-only rows (no steppers, no select buttons; rooms are chosen in checkout). Hotels add a "Select room" button (then a stepper, `RoomPicker`) to each available room once it is priced; sold-out rooms have none. The quantities add up to the rooms searched, a room that turns sold out is dropped from the choice, and the choice lives only in the current visit: it is never saved or restored, so rooms are never selected for the visitor. Changing the dates or the travellers (rooms, adults, children or a child's age) starts the choice again; flexibility does not. Every priced room shows "From $X per night / per person".
- **Search line:** once rooms are loaded, on experience pages a line says which search the prices are for, from the search the rooms answer (never the draft in the form), in the booking card (and the mobile sheet) and instead of the "All prices are…" note in the Rooms tab: experiences "Prices for 12–19 Mar · 3 travellers, per person. You'll choose your room at checkout.", below 640px the short form "Prices for 12–19 Mar · 3 travellers. Choose your room at checkout.". Hotels show no such line (the Rooms tab keeps "All prices are per room, per night."). Hidden while loading. Dates use `formatDateRange` (the visitor's locale, year only when not the current one, en dash, month collapsed); wording in `src/helpers/search-summary.ts`. The Rooms tab's live region announces "Rooms updated. <line>".
- **Hotel booking card:** under that line it lists the rooms chosen ("1 of 2 rooms selected", each with its nightly price) and, once complete, the total for the stay; before any choice it says "Select your rooms in the Rooms tab.". "Reserve now" stays off until the rooms chosen add up to the rooms searched and hold every guest (the note says what is missing) and the checkout link carries `selection`. Experiences keep "Prices for your dates. Rooms are confirmed at checkout."
- Rooms tab (all pages), unchanged: Notes when idle: "Rooms depend on your dates. The room types below are shown for reference. Availability and prices are confirmed after you select Check availability." and "All prices are per person, based on shared occupancy." (hotel: "All prices are per room, per night.", see assumptions).
- After the first load started from "Check availability" the page switches to `#rooms` and scrolls there (`markReveal`/`consumeReveal`). Arriving with dates in the URL does not switch tabs or scroll. A polite live region says "Rooms updated" after each answer.
- **Date picker:** it closes by itself once the range is complete (experiences: right after the start date, the end follows) and focus returns to the date field; availability then loads at once.
- **Mobile (below `lg`):** the bar button follows the same state as the booking card through one hook (`useBookingState`, `src/hooks/use-booking-state.ts`): "Select dates" (opens the sheet), "Checking…" (disabled), "Reserve now" (goes to checkout; opens the sheet when there is no checkout) and a disabled "Reserve now" when nothing is available. The bar says what the card says, from the same stores (`barSummary`, `src/helpers/booking-bar-summary.ts`, rendered by `BookingBarSummary`): with a valid range, one summary button (a border, a chevron, pressed and focus states, 64px high; its accessible name is its visible text followed by "Change dates and guests", never an aria-label that leaves the text out) with the dates and the travellers, for **hotels** a rooms line (what to do while no room is chosen, then "N of M rooms selected" and the rooms one per line, two at most and "+N more", then what is still to do; only the count in landscape; none under 360px wide) and, on the right above the button (outside the summary button), the lowest price per night or per person, and once a hotel's rooms are chosen "Total for N nights"; loading shows a skeleton and "Checking…"; no room shows "Not available for your dates". **Experiences and packages never show the rooms line or a total** (they choose rooms in checkout). With no valid dates there is no price: the bar is one full-width "Select dates" button with a calendar icon that opens the dates. A search that is not valid yet (a child without an age) says what to do and the button reads "Review search". Tapping the summary (or the button) opens the sheet with the panel that needs attention open (`useBookingState().panel`, passed through `BookingSheetIntentContext` to `BookingForm`): the dates when there are none or the stay is out of its limits, the travellers when they are the problem, else nothing. The bar is at least 5rem high plus the bottom safe area and as tall as its content (a ResizeObserver in `MobileBookingBar` publishes `--booking-bar-h`, which the page spacer and the footer spacer use), so the page and the footer are never covered. The button that opened the sheet gets the focus back when it closes. The booking sheet stays open after the picker closes and shows "Reserve now" once rooms are loaded; the switch to the Rooms tab waits while the sheet is open (`availabilityStore.holdReveal`) and happens when it closes.
- **Child ages:** there is no default age. A new child shows "Select age"; a missing age (also in the URL) makes the search invalid: no fetch, an inline message ("Select the age of each child.") and the dates are kept. A missing age is written to the URL as an empty entry.
- **Group limits:** an experience allows adults up to its group maximum (12, `EXPERIENCE_GROUP_SIZE`; `experienceStayLimits`, `guestLimitsForPath`); hotels keep 9. `TODO(api)`: the limit per experience comes with the product, not the path.
- **Sold-out rows:** a tinted card, the photo greyed under a crossed-bed icon and "Sold out", a rounded "SOLD OUT" pill in the price column (never wrapped; "for your dates" for screen readers), and text at 4.5:1 or better.
- "Reserve now" opens `ROUTES.checkout` with `type`, `id`, `checkIn`, `checkOut`, `rooms`, `adults`, `children`, `childAges` (and `flex`); see `src/helpers/checkout-params.ts`. Without the checkout flag it shows "Online booking opens soon."

## When it fetches
- Only for a complete, valid request (`toRoomRequest`, `src/helpers/availability-request.ts`): dates set, every child has an age 0-17, rooms <= adults, and for an experience its fixed nights and the maximum group size.
- **Fetching happens when an edit is committed, not on each change:** while the dates or travellers panel is open nothing is asked (`availabilityStore.beginEdit()`, held by the booking form for as long as a panel is open) and the page keeps what it shows. It asks once the panel closes: Done, an outside click or Escape, or the date picker closing itself when the range is complete. A link or saved search asks at once. No call without dates. A search identical to the last one is skipped, so an edit that ends where it began asks nothing. The URL follows the committed search. Stale responses are aborted or ignored. Results are cached per request for the session.
- First load: the search is read from the URL, then from the saved stay (`vku:stay:<path>`, 24 h). If valid, the controller fetches at once on the client after hydration; the server and the hydration render are always the idle state (skeletons from the first client render when the search is valid happen as soon as the controller asks). Invalid or missing: idle (invalid URL dates are dropped).
- Never on the server: pages do not read `searchParams`, so the HTML is identical for every search (e2e checks it). Canonical excludes search params, and dated URLs are noindex,follow by header (`FILTER_PARAMS` includes the stay keys and `mock`); the sitemap lists clean URLs only.
- The URL follows the search with `history.replaceState` (no history entry; other params and the hash are kept; removed when the dates are cleared).

## Code
- Service: `getRoomAvailability(request, { signal, mock })` in `src/services/room-availability.ts` calls `GET /api/rooms/availability` (`src/app/api/rooms/availability/route.ts`, validates, `no-store`), which calls `IAvailabilityService.getRoomAvailability` (mock in `src/mocks/services/availability.mock.ts`: 1 s delay, scenarios). `TODO(api)`: replace the mock with the real endpoint; request `{ kind, id, checkIn, checkOut, adults, children, childAges, rooms }`, response `{ rooms: [{ roomId, available, price: Money, unit: "night" | "person" }] }`. In real mode keep only a short minimum loading time (`minLoadingMs`, 300 ms) against flicker.
- State: `src/store/availability-store.ts` (debounce, abort, cache, identical-skip), `src/store/stay-store.ts` (dates, flex, hydrate from URL then saved stay), `src/helpers/stay-url.ts` (URL read/write). The steppers, child ages and flexibility chips are shared components in `src/components/ui/guest-fields.tsx` (also used by the search box).
- UI: `AvailabilityController` (`src/features/availability/`, renders nothing), `BookingForm`, `BookingAvailabilityPrice`, `RoomsPanel`, `RoomRow`.
- Types: `RoomAvailabilityRequest`, `RoomAvailabilityResponse`, `RoomAvailability`, `MockScenario` in `src/interfaces/detail.ts`; `priceFrom` / `price` are optional on `HotelDetail`, `ExperienceDetail`, `HotelRoom` and cards. `productJsonLd` writes no `offers` without a price.

## Mock scenarios (dev only: `USE_MOCKS=true` and not production)
Add to the page URL: `?mock=available` (default: two room types are sold out, picked from the hotel and dates so the same search always gives the same rooms, and at least one stays available), `?mock=partial` (every second room sold out), `?mock=none` (all sold out), `?mock=error` (the call fails). The parameter is ignored in production and is part of `FILTER_PARAMS` (canonical stays clean).

## Assumptions and open items
- Hotel prices are per room per night (the spec text said per person); experiences are per person.
- Listing, category and home cards are unchanged; only related cards on detail pages hide the price.
- Legacy selection components (`RoomPicker`, `RoomsSummaryBar`, `ReserveGate`, `BookingPrice`, `ExperienceBookingPrice`, the server availability panels, `RoomCheckoutLink`) stay exported but are no longer rendered.
